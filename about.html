// About Ad-Spot

A simpler board for buying and selling.

Ad-Spot started as a class project with one goal: make it easy to post an ad and just as easy to find one, without the clutter of a typical classifieds site.

What we're building

A responsive, web-based advertising platform where individuals and businesses can list and discover products and services.

What matters to us

The principles behind how Ad-Spot is designed.

Clarity first

Every ad shows the price, location, and category up front, no digging required.

Fast to search

Keyword, category, and location filters work together instantly as you type.

Built for every screen

The same experience adapts cleanly from a small phone to a wide desktop monitor.

Honest feedback

Forms tell you exactly what to fix, in plain language, the moment something's wrong.
